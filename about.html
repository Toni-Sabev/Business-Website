Какво правим

From Bulgaria to a US university — and everything in between.

We help Bulgarian students get from Bulgaria to universities in the United States. From the first conversation to graduation day, we work as long-term educational and developmental consultants — not a one-time application service.

Нашите пакети:

Надежда

Първата стъпка към американски университет.

Услуги:

  • Изисквания за документация, критерии за прием и преглед на процеса на кандидатстване
  • Проучване на университети, изграждане на профил и пряка комуникация
  • Анализ на разходите, финансово планиране и логистична координация (родители)
Възраст: 15–17 г. 9–24 месеца процес
Насока

Всичко от Надежда + насока през целия студентски престой.

Допълнителни услуги:

  • Транспорт, настаняване и адаптация при пристигане
  • Насока за избор на специалност и професионално развитие
  • Насока по време на следването — данъци, здравни осигуровки, документация
Възраст: 15–22 г. Процес 3–5 години
Успехът Очаквайте

Всичко от Надежда и Насока + подготовка за работната сфера.

  • Подготовка за професионалния живот — стажове и корпоративен етикет
  • Подпомагане със CV-та, интервюта и кариерно развитие
  • Насока за магистратура, сертификати и следващи стъпки

Simeon Sabev

Simeon has been an academic and athletic asset to numerous organizations. Co-Founder

Kaloyan Levterov

BS · Global Enterprise Management Financial Data Analyst · MSCI Olympian · Tokyo 2020 Bronze Medalist · Euro Champs Multi-National

Kaloyan Levterov has been a dominant force in the development of Bulgarian swimming. At age 17, he qualified for the 2020 Tokyo Olympic Games, and at 18, he claimed the bronze medal at the European Junior Championships in Rome, Italy. After securing multiple medals, finals, and accolades alongside the fastest swimmers on the planet, Kaloyan had to make a strategic decision to advance his career. His initial commitment to Texas A&M University was marked by growth and early success. After his first year, Kaloyan made a deliberate move to the University of Delaware, where the three founders graduated and developed professionally. Currently, Kaloyan is building a career in data analysis and global business management, joining Simeon as a Financial Data Analyst at MSCI.

  • BS — Global Enterprise Management
  • Olympian — represented Bulgaria at Tokyo 2020 Summer Olympics, 100m & 200m backstroke
  • Multi-time National Record Holder
  • Bronze medalist — LEN European Junior Championships (Rome 2021)
  • Multi-time All-CAA honoree — individual & relay events
  • Multi-time European Men's Finalist
  • 3 World Championships · 7 European Championships represented Bulgaria
  • Financial Data Analyst, MSCI (Morgan Stanley Capital International)

Tonislav Sabev

BS · Management Information Systems Data Analyst · Bloomberg NCAA Qualifier National Record Holder Multi-National

Tonislav Sabev graduated from the Mathematics High School in Veliko Tarnovo, before continuing his education at the University of Delaware in the United States. During his time there, Tonislav competed at a high level in swimming, qualifying for the NCAA National University Championships and reaching the semi-finals at the Men's European Championships in Rome. Alongside his athletic achievements, he built a strong academic foundation in technology and finance, specialising in Information Systems with a concentration in Finance. Today, Tonislav works as a Data Analyst at Bloomberg, where he focuses on the automated processing and analysis of large volumes of data.

  • BS — Finance & Management Information Systems
  • National Record Holder — 50m breaststroke
  • First swimmer in University of Delaware history to compete at the NCAA Championships
