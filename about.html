What We Do

From Bulgaria to a US university — and everything in between.

We help Bulgarian students get from Bulgaria to universities in the United States. From the first conversation to graduation day, we work as long-term educational and developmental consultants — not a one-time application service.

What we cover:

University Selection

Matching each student to schools that fit their academic profile, ambitions, and family budget.

Budgeting & Financial Planning

Realistic cost mapping, scholarship strategy, and financial aid — no surprises.

Major & Specialization Guidance

Helping students pick a field that aligns with their strengths and the realities of the US job market.

Housing

On-campus, off-campus, and the full logistics of moving abroad.

In-School Consulting

Ongoing support throughout the academic year: course selection, internships, career planning, and adjusting to life in the US.

We take a holistic approach to each student. Parents are trusting us with their child's most formative years, and we treat that responsibility accordingly.
